# Project story

The narrative version of Switchyard: why it exists, what problem it
targets, and how it evolved. This is the document to hand someone who
wants the story in five minutes rather than the commit history.

Written and updated as the project actually progresses — not written in
advance and not backfilled with invented history.

## What belongs here

- The motivating problem and why it's worth building (not just claiming)
  a routing system to study it.
- The research question, stated plainly.
- A short account of how the approach changed as real results came in
  (what was tried, what worked, what was dropped) — pointing to
  `DECISIONS.md` and `FAILURES_AND_LESSONS.md` for detail rather than
  duplicating it.
- The current state of the project and what it demonstrates so far.
